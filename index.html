Meet the Detective

Thirty-Eight Years on the Beat.
Zero Days Off.

Kathryn has lived in Prestolee for thirty-eight years, with a front-row view of Stoneclough and the M62. In that time she has attended forty-one funerals, twenty-seven weddings (she knows which ones were "arranged"), and one very awkward barbecue where she overheard absolutely everything.

She is not a private investigator in any official sense. She has no licence, no office, and no case files — they're all in her head, filed alphabetically by how embarrassing they are. She works alone — officially. Unofficially there is Julie: surveillance partner, note-taker, and the reason Kathryn has never once been caught at a fence. But ask anyone in Stoneclough or Prestolee who really runs this patch, and you'll get one answer.

Her methods are unorthodox. Her accuracy is 98%. The other 2% she has never once admitted to.

Field Manual

Investigative Methods

No technology. No gadgets. Just a woman, a dog, and an unreasonable amount of free time.

01

Curtain Surveillance

Every window in the street has been catalogued. Kathryn can tell you what's on your TV, who's visiting, and whether the curtains are "the thin ones" — which they are.

02

Bin Archaeology

The bin bags don't lie. She can tell you what you had for dinner, whether you're on a diet, and that the "healthy eating" phase lasted exactly nine days.

03

The Garden Gate Trick

She is always just passing by. Always. The gate has never once been closed for a reason, and she has never once been "just passing by" in the same direction twice.

04

Dog-Walking Reconnaissance

Biscuit walks the perimeter at 7am sharp. Kathryn calls it "a walk." It is, in fact, a patrol with a tail.

05

The Fence Eavesdrop

Her ears are her best asset. She has never once been caught listening to a conversation she wasn't part of. (She has.)

06

The Two-Woman Stroll

Kathryn and Julie "just happen" to be walking the same direction at the same time. Every day. For years. The neighbours have stopped asking.
